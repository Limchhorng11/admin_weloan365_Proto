## Announcement — new content type under Media

New **Announcement** section under Media, positioned below CSR (`/content/announcement`).
Reuses the shared Post editor — bilingual Khmer/English authoring, cover media,
scheduling — for system notices (holidays, maintenance windows, policy updates) shown
to customers in the mobile app.

- No Category or Location — both are hidden in the New/Edit modal, the Category
  column is hidden in the list table, and the category filter-chip row is hidden.
  Blog Posts and CSR are unaffected.
- Seeded with three sample posts covering all three statuses (Published, Scheduled,
  Failed).

**Files touched:** `lib/data.ts`, `app/content/announcement/page.tsx` (new),
`components/sidebar.tsx`, `components/topbar.tsx`, `components/posts-manager.tsx`

## Cover media upload — Blog Posts / CSR / Announcement

- **CSR** — multi-image/video gallery, capped at **10** items, "N/10 used" counter,
  "Add more" tile hides at the cap. Empty-state: *"Up to 10 images / videos, ~5 MB
  each."*
- **Blog Posts** and **Announcement** — single image or video only; picking a new
  file replaces the current one (Replace/Remove actions). Empty-state: *"One image
  or video, ~5 MB."*
- **Blog Posts only** additionally has an **"Or paste a YouTube link"** input below
  the upload box. Accepts `watch?v=`, `youtu.be/`, `embed/`, and `shorts/` URLs;
  renders a real YouTube thumbnail with a play overlay everywhere cover media
  displays (editor preview, list-table thumbnail, full Preview pane). Not available
  on CSR or Announcement.

**Files touched:** `lib/data.ts`, `components/posts-manager.tsx`

## Promotion editor

- **Bilingual authoring:** Title and Description are now Khmer/English (`{ km, en }`),
  with the same language-tab row as the Post editor — Khmer required, English
  optional — at the top of the form.
- **Thumbnail + Detail image:** the single Image field is now two independent
  uploads — **Thumbnail** (required, image only, shown on the promotion card/list)
  and **Detail image** (optional, image only, shown on the promotion's own detail
  page), each with its own Replace/Remove.
- **Layout:** matches the wide 2-column layout used by Blog Posts/CSR/Announcement —
  Title/Description/Image in the main column, Choice button/Schedule post/Set
  deadline in the sidebar.
- **Sidebar order:** Choice button → **Schedule post** → **Set deadline**.

**Files touched:** `lib/data.ts`, `app/content/promotions/page.tsx`

## CSR post editor — language tabs at the top

The Khmer/English language tabs now render first in the New/Edit post form, ahead
of the CSR quotation field. Blog Posts and Announcement are unaffected (they don't
have a quotation field).

**Files touched:** `components/posts-manager.tsx`

## Sidebar — Chat unread indicator on the icon

The red unread-activity dot for the Chat nav item is now a small badge overlaid on
the top-right corner of the chat icon itself, instead of a separate dot floating
after the "Chat" label text.

**Files touched:** `components/sidebar.tsx`
